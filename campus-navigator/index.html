Drawn Directly From Official Floor Plan

The interactive 2D map above was drafted based directly on this official Ground Floor Plan mounted at the Department of Mechanical Engineering entrance lobby at Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research (AITR), Indore.

  • North Orientation: Oriented with North pointing to the right, matching the blueprint's original arrow marker.
  • Front Corridor & Lawns: Reflects the three manicured lawns, two pedestrian access pathways, and main access road.
  • 3 Academic Wings: Captures the angled layout of Block 1 (Auditorium & Workshop), Block 2 (IBM, SKF & AICTE Labs), and Block 3 (Core Mechanical & Automotive Labs).
  • Exact Dimensions: Preserves all architectural room dimensions (e.g. 61'2" × 34'2", 26'10" × 34'0", 41'10" × 39'6").
